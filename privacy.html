Privacy Policy

This policy explains how TubeGrowth collects, uses, and protects information when you use our site and services.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to provide, secure, and improve our services. Cookies help remember preferences, provide analytics, and enable advertising features. You can control cookie settings through your browser.

Google AdSense & Advertising

We display ads on our site using Google AdSense and other advertising partners. Ads may be personalised using information collected by us and third-party advertisers. If you prefer not to receive personalized ads, you can opt out via Google Ad Settings.

Third‑Party Advertising

We allow third‑party advertising networks (including Google) to serve ads on our site. These third parties may use cookies and other tracking technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites to provide targeted advertising.

Data Collection & Use

We collect information you provide directly (for example, when you contact support), information about your use of the site (analytics), and technical data (such as browser and device information). We use this information to operate the site, improve our services, provide support, and comply with legal obligations.

Analytics Tools

We use analytics tools such as Google Analytics to understand site usage and improve performance. These tools collect usage data (page views, time on site, referral sources) and may set cookies. Analytics data is aggregated and used for product improvements.

Contact & Data Requests

If you have questions about this policy or want to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, please contact us at support@tubegrowth.com.

  • Basic technical data such as browser type and device information

How we use information
